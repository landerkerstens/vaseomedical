About Vaseo Medical

A specialist distributor, built around one system.

Vaseo Medical Pty Ltd is the Australian arm of Prion Medical, founded in Belgium and active across Belgium and the Netherlands. Prion Medical was built on a deliberately narrow idea: represent few technologies, and understand each one as well as the clinicians using it.

In Australia we supply a single system — the Cambridge Interventional CRF radiofrequency ablation platform and its internally-cooled electrodes. One generator, one electrode range, and a widening set of indications behind it. Stock, training, case support and funding guidance are all organised around that one system, working directly alongside the endocrine surgeons, gynaecologists, interventional radiologists and orthopaedic teams who use it.

Regulatory & quality

Australian compliance, by design.

Medical devices supplied in Australia are regulated by the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA). Vaseo Medical Pty Ltd is committed to supplying the CRF ablation system and its electrodes only where they are appropriately included in the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG) for their intended use, and to operating in line with applicable Australian regulatory and quality requirements. Indications differ in how far they are established — talk to us about where a given application stands before you plan around it.
